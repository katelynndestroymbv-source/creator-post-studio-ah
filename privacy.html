Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Creator Post Studio AH (“we”, “the Service”) helps TikTok creators preview, schedule and publish their own videos after review. This policy explains what data we access, how we use it, and how you can control it.

1. Data we access

When you connect your TikTok account and approve the requested scopes, we may access:

  • Basic profile: your TikTok display name, avatar and app-scoped user ID, to show who is logged in.
  • Your videos: your video list and metadata, to show your own content and publishing status.
  • Content you provide: videos, titles, privacy settings, duet / stitch settings and commercial content disclosures you enter for publishing.
  • Technical data: basic logs needed to operate the Service, such as publishing status and error logs.

We do not access your advertising data, your private messages, or other users’ data.

2. How we use data

We use data only to operate the Service: to display your profile and videos, to let you preview and confirm posts, to send your confirmed post to TikTok via the Content Posting API, and to show publishing status. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Storage and retention

We retain only what is needed to operate the Service. Videos you upload for publishing are used for that publishing request and for status checks. You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time, which stops future access.

4. Sharing

We share data with TikTok only as needed to perform the publishing action you confirmed. We do not share your data with other third parties except as required by law.

5. Your choices

You can disconnect your TikTok account from the Service or from your TikTok settings at any time. You can also request deletion of data associated with your use of the Service by contacting us through the method listed in the TikTok Developer Portal for this app.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including encrypted transport (HTTPS) and access controls. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Children

The Service is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law. Do not use the Service if you do not meet TikTok’s age requirements.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method listed in the TikTok Developer Portal for this app.
