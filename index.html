Creator Post Studio AH

Help creators preview, schedule and publish TikTok videos from one workspace, with review before posting.

Terms of Service Privacy Policy

What it does

Creator Post Studio AH is a web tool for TikTok creators. Connect your TikTok account, upload a video, preview the title, privacy setting, duet / stitch options and commercial content disclosure, then schedule or publish — only after you review and confirm.

  • Log in with TikTok Login Kit
  • View your profile and your videos
  • Upload videos to drafts or publish directly, after your own review
  • See publishing status for each post

How publishing works

  1. You log in with your TikTok account and authorise only the scopes needed for login, viewing your videos, and posting.
  2. You upload a video and fill in the title, privacy level, duet / stitch settings, and commercial content disclosure.
  3. You preview everything and confirm. Nothing is posted without your explicit confirmation.
  4. You can choose Direct Post, or Upload to Inbox as a draft to finish in the TikTok app.

We do not post automatically, we do not access advertising data, and we do not use your data for advertising.

Data and safety

We only use the data needed to show your own profile, your videos, and your publishing status. You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for details.
